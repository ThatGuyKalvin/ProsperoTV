## New since 01.000.080

### Watching

- **The TV's own remote.** With **Settings > System > HDMI > Enable HDMI Device Link** on (and the TV's CEC setting), the television's remote works in the menu and the player, beside the controller or instead of it. Arrows move, OK selects, Back goes back, Play / Pause plays or pauses, Rewind and Fast forward page through lists and step through a video, Channel − / + change the channel on live TV, and Menu opens the playback panel. Which keys reach the console is up to the television; arrows, OK and Back always do. The README has the full table.
- **Reaching the tabs.** Up from the top of a screen, or Circle on Live TV, focuses the tab bar; Left and Right change the tab there, and Down, Cross or Circle go back in. Left of Home is a Search button, and Left from the search field reaches the results.
- **Cross pauses.** During playback Cross pauses and resumes, as OK on a TV remote does; in the open channel list it still watches the channel. L2 now opens and closes the live channel list, and the phone remote has a **Channel list** button.

### Sources

- **Films and shows counted.** The Sources panel, the Settings tab and its At a glance panel show how many films and shows a source holds beside its channels, each on its own line in Sources. The channel figure counts live channels only, so a playlist's films and episodes no longer inflate it, and the header chip gives the total of all three. The counts refresh with the catalog and after an On Demand download finishes.

### Fixes

- **A channel in every category it belongs to.** Newer Xtream panels can file a live channel under several categories, and the app only read the first, so a category made up of such channels was missing from the list and from search. Every category the provider names now lists the channel, and a channel in any adult category is treated as an adult channel.

### Maintenance

- The interface kit, the OpenGL SDK and the owned-root helper are fetched from LedyBacer's copies of their original repositories, which are gone; the pinned commits and checksums are unchanged.

## Validation notes

Host tests cover the TV remote reader and its key mapping, tab focus and the search button, Cross and L2 in the player, the film and show counts read from saved lists and playlists, and Xtream channels with several categories.

## Installing by hand

- `PPSA99003.zip`: extract it and upload the complete `PPSA99003` folder to `/data/homebrew`, replacing the old one after closing the app. Keep your existing data under `/data/prosperotv`.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the checksum of the download.
- Coming from 01.000.015 or older: close ProsperoTV, delete `PPSA99003.ffpfsc` from `/data/homebrew` if you used the image, then upload the folder and restart ShadowMountPlus or the console.

Release files are built by this repository's GitHub Actions from the version tag on the release commit from `main`.
