## New since 01.000.050

### Home and browsing

- **Home screen:** return to what you were watching, your favorites, and programmes on your usual channels.
- **One search:** find live channels, movies, TV shows, and guide programmes from the same search box.
- **Organize channels:** rename, reorder, and hide individual channels and arrange favorites. Browse recently added and most-watched lists, and use provider channel numbers with the number pad.
- **Movie and show details:** see provider posters, plots, cast, year, and ratings, with sorting and genre/year filters.
- **On-demand counts:** Movies and TV shows display the number of available titles for the selected source. Empty lists show **0**, are grayed out, and cannot open. Loading and failed requests are not mistaken for zero; show counts count shows, not episodes.

### Watching

- **Continue watching:** resume movies and episodes, and continue to the next episode when one finishes.
- **Playback controls:** seek within supported on-demand videos, jump to chapters where available, change playback speed, and adjust sound delay.
- **Smoother seeking:** the last picture stays visible while seeking or changing speed instead of switching to black. A buffering message appears until playback resumes. The HUD shows elapsed/total time and a progress bar, and a non-default speed such as **2x** remains visible after the full controls hide.
- **Picture controls:** preserve the video's aspect ratio by default; choose Fit, Stretch, forced 4:3 or 16:9, and zoom/fill. Adjust subtitle size and color.
- **Picture in picture:** keep a channel playing in a corner while browsing.
- **USB recording and guide reminders:** record live TV or schedule recordings and reminders from the guide. Keep the app running; recording uses an additional provider connection.
- **HLS quality selection:** choose a rendition manually, or select Auto to reduce quality when compatible streams repeatedly buffer. Auto reduces quality only and requires usable rendition bitrates.

### Sound

- **Optional 5.1 surround:** Settings now offers Stereo (the default) or decoded 5.1 PCM for compatible HDMI equipment. AAC, AC-3, and E-AC-3 preserve six channels through playback speed, volume, and normalization, with stereo fallback when surround output fails. This is PCM output, not Dolby/DTS bitstream passthrough.
- **Louder, clearer stereo downmix:** 5.1 sources no longer lose about 7.65 dB when mixed to stereo. A peak limiter controls clipping, and AC-3/E-AC-3 diagnostics now report their decoded channel count correctly.

### Sources and provider compatibility

- **Mixed M3U/M3U Plus playlists:** live channels stay in Live TV; recognized movies and episodes appear in On demand. Conventional episode names are grouped into shows, and stream request headers and alternate addresses are preserved.
- **More Xtream responses accepted:** UTF-8 byte-order marks, nullable fields, escaped Unicode controls, and indexed season arrays are handled correctly.
- **Source request settings:** edit the User-Agent, referrer, and custom HTTP headers. Choose app-default, Smarters, VLC, or custom User-Agent presets without typing a full value.
- **USB playlists:** import a playlist from a USB drive.
- **Custom programme guides:** set an XMLTV address and map its entries to your channels.
- **Xtream account information:** view expiry and connection limits, with an expiry notice.
- **Background source checks:** check channels and mark those that do not open.

### Household, phone, and interface

- **Phone browsing:** browse the catalog and guide, search, and select playback from the paired phone remote.
- **Viewing limits:** set a daily allowance and bedtime for kids' mode.
- **Encrypted console sharing:** transfer sources and favorites over the home network, with certificate fingerprint verification and approval on the receiving console.
- **Arabic and Hebrew:** shaped text and right-to-left layouts, a saved language override, and expanded guide/recording translations.
- **Appearance and accessibility:** themes, larger text, high contrast, and an idle/paused screensaver.
- **Diagnostics:** best-effort crash recovery reports and the actual profile-specific debug-log path displayed in Settings.

### Performance and maintenance

- Fixed pronounced stuttering when scrolling the large iptv-org public catalog, including held L2/R2 paging. Tested scrolling frame windows improved from roughly 50–52 ms to 17–18 ms; occasional frame-time spikes can still occur.
- Empty on-demand catalogs are now cached correctly, so returning from a refresh does not restore an old title count.
- Improved large-catalog category layout performance, corrected native diagnostic tab labels, and fixed host sanitizer/font ownership checks.
- GitHub CI now retains separate compiler caches for its two build jobs, with reusable cache snapshots. Added a public roadmap for future work.

## Validation notes

Host tests cover the new library, playback, provider, translation, and household behavior. Console checks cover navigation, live playback, VOD seeks and speed changes, public-catalog scrolling, empty VOD buttons, and decoded surround output with clean shutdown.

Physical 5.1 speaker routing has not been verified with a receiver. The newer scheduled USB recording, encrypted two-console sharing, and automatic quality reduction have not completed end-to-end hardware acceptance. Crash reports cannot capture power loss or every forced termination. Spoken-focus accessibility is not included.

## Installing by hand

- `PPSA99003.zip`: extract it and upload the complete `PPSA99003` folder to `/data/homebrew`, replacing the old one after closing the app. Keep your existing data under `/data/prosperotv`.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the checksum of the download.
- Coming from 01.000.015 or older: close ProsperoTV, delete `PPSA99003.ffpfsc` from `/data/homebrew` if you used the image, then upload the folder and restart ShadowMountPlus or the console.

Release files are built by this repository's GitHub Actions from the version tag on the release commit from `main`.
