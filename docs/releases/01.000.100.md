## New since 01.000.090

### On Demand

- **Poster grids.** Movies and TV shows open in a poster grid, in the same Dusk colours as the other tabs, with the focused title's details beside it. Cross watches a film or opens a show's seasons and episodes; titles without artwork get a readable placeholder. Touchpad opens the categories, Square a title's details, Triangle Search and R3 the filters and sorting; L2 and R2 turn pages.
- **A–Z bar.** Right past the last poster in a row focuses a letter bar; Up and Down jump between the letters that have titles, and Cross, Left or Circle go back to the posters. It works in Movies, TV shows and their categories, keeps the current category and filters, and switches to Title A–Z order without losing the focused title. Numbers and other scripts are under **#**.

### Search

- **Every saved source.** Search finds channels, films, shows and programmes across all saved sources, with the provider's name beside each result. A result from another source switches to it before opening. Other sources are searched from their saved catalogs and guides in the background, so refresh a source and open its Movies and TV shows once to include them. Hidden channels and categories, parental controls and Kids mode apply to every source.

### Guide

- **Guide timing.** In Sources, focus a saved source and press **R3** to correct its guide clock or its catch-up clock, separately, in 30-minute steps up to 12 hours either way. Cross resets a correction. The guide correction applies on Home, in search, the guide and the playback banner; saved guides keep the provider's original times, so a correction never adds up across refreshes. Corrections are kept per source and per profile, and are protected by parental controls.

### About

- **Collaborators.** The About tab lists the project's collaborators under their own heading, below the thanks.

### Fixes

- **USB playlists.** Importing an M3U from a USB drive no longer crashes the app, or every launch after it. The safety checks on the file are kept.
- **Older system software.** On system software 3.00, the app could fail to start because the video decoder was set up before graphics. Graphics now start first.

### Maintenance

- `tools/dev.sh` (macOS, Linux) and `tools/dev.ps1` (Windows) build, test, preview and deploy the app in a container with the CI toolchain, using Podman or Docker. `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md` covers the setup on each system.

## Validation notes

Host tests cover the poster grids and A–Z jumps, search across saved sources, guide and catch-up corrections with their persistence, source isolation, offline restarts and parental controls, and the start-up order on older system software.

## Installing by hand

- `PPSA99003.zip`: extract it and upload the complete `PPSA99003` folder to `/data/homebrew`, replacing the old one after closing the app. Keep your existing data under `/data/prosperotv`.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the checksum of the download.
- Coming from 01.000.015 or older: close ProsperoTV, delete `PPSA99003.ffpfsc` from `/data/homebrew` if you used the image, then upload the folder and restart ShadowMountPlus or the console.

Release files are built by this repository's GitHub Actions from the version tag on the release commit from `main`.
